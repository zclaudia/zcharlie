# Ningbo FY2025 asset-quality ratios: rating-report scope evidence

**Conclusion.** The official ChinaMoney attachment for China Chengxin International Credit Rating Co., Ltd. (中诚信国际, CCXI), *宁波银行股份有限公司2026年第一期无固定期限资本债券信用评级报告及跟踪评级安排*, directly labels its FY2025 ratio table “宁波银行（合并口径）” and reports NPL ratio 0.76% and NPL provision coverage 373.16%. This is a strong, direct consolidated-scope source for both requested FY2025 values. It is an independent rating-agency report, not a statement authored by Ningbo Bank. CCXI says its analysis used data provided by the bank and identifies the 2025 financial report as EY-audited with a standard unmodified opinion; the 2025 column is the 2025 audited report's period-end data.

The report's printed page 4 has the explicitly consolidated summary table. The 2025 column shows NPL balance RMB 13,147 hundred-million yuan, NPL ratio 0.76%, and coverage 373.16%. Its notes define the loan-loss allowance as including both amortized-cost and FVOCI loan allowances, state that coverage and allowance/total-loans follow annual-report disclosure basis, and say 2025 figures are period-end amounts from the audited 2025 financial report. The report says the analysis is based on information provided by Ningbo Bank; it identifies EY as auditor and the opinion as standard unmodified. The PDF's printed pages 15–16 are Appendix 2, “主要财务数据（合并口径）”: 2025 gross loans RMB 17,333.14 hundred-million yuan, allowance RMB 491.81 hundred-million yuan, five-category NPL balance RMB 131.47 hundred-million yuan, and total assets RMB 36,286.71 hundred-million yuan. Printed pages 16–17 are Appendix 3, “主要财务指标（合并口径),” and repeat both 2025 ratios. Printed page 18 gives the agency's definitions: NPL ratio = five-category NPL balance / total loans; coverage = loan-loss allowance / NPL balance.

**Boundary of what this proves.** The consolidated scope of the two reported ratios is explicit and applies on the same table, with a second explicit consolidated appendix. The report does not independently reconstruct the coverage numerator used for the published 373.16%. Its consolidated allowance line of RMB 491.81 hundred-million yuan divided by its RMB 131.47 hundred-million-yuan NPL balance does not equal 373.16%; CCXI explicitly calls coverage an annual-report-disclosure-basis metric. Thus this source closes the scope question, but it is not a stand-alone arithmetic bridge from the appendix allowance amount to the regulatory coverage figure. Preserve the annual-report disclosure-basis note when using the ratio; do not substitute the agency's generic formula or infer a numerator from rounded values.
