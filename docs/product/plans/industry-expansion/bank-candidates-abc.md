# ABC FY2025 bank candidate evidence

This report covers Agricultural Bank of China (601288.SH): weighted-average ROE 10.16%, net interest margin 1.28%, NPL ratio 1.27%, and provision coverage 292.55%. Candidate records, page excerpts, hashes, calculations, and report-specific bridge evidence are in [`records.json`](../../../../research/industry-data-validation/evidence/bank-candidates-2026-10-15/abc/records.json). The source is the official CNINFO FY2025 annual report, SHA-256 `9353f9a05d9cdf19cabef785e4c633fbd29ab63e7933dcd3c472ca02bc691d83`.

The underlying indicator values are on PDF p11, with 2025/2024/2023 columns and definitions in notes 3, 4, 8, and 9. They are accepted as group-consolidated only through the narrowly scoped checks in `investment_agent.industry_peers.bank_abc_bridges.checked_abc_scope_bridge`; this helper is pinned to the exact issuer, FY2025 report identity, PDF hash, excerpts, and calculations. It does not establish a report-wide group default or generalize to another report.
